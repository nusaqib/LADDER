# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be professional and respectful; no harassment, personal
attacks, or discrimination; assume good faith; disagree about
engineering, not about people. Controls engineering has a safety culture
for a reason — the same culture applies to how we review each other's
work: findings are stated plainly, disposed honestly, and never made
personal.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers (see repository owner
contact). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn,
moderate, or ban accounts that violate this code, and will recuse
themselves from cases in which they are involved.
